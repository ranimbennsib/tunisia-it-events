About

A home for Tunisia’s tech community

Tunisia IT Events brings together the hackathons, workshops, conferences, trainings and meetups that happen across the country. Organizers publish their events here, and every event is checked by an admin before it goes live.

Participants can search by city, domain and type, register in a few clicks, pay online when an event is paid, and keep every receipt in their own space.

For participants

  • Search and filter all approved events
  • Register, and pay by card for paid events
  • Save favorites and print your receipts
Create an account

For organizers

  • Publish events with image, price and capacity
  • See participants and payments per event
  • Follow what has been paid out to you
Become an organizer
